# The Red Pitch

Teil von [[Englisch]].

## The Plot

The story takes place in urban south London, inside of a football pitch "The Red Pitch". Bilal, Omz and Joey are three boys, who love football and are aiming for becoming professional football players in the future. All three get selected for the trials at QPR. In the end only one of them makes it to the team.

Although the two other boys seem to be delighted by their friend's success at first, one of them expresses his jealousy deliberately fouling his friend. The situation triggers in a fight between the boys since the third one aids his suffering friend. The repercussion of the dispute is a fight that takes long to reconcile.
